# SARUS+ Community

**[Русский](README.md)** · **[English](README.en.md)**

An independent, non-commercial community around the commercial PLM system **SARUS+** (САРУС+).

This GitHub organization collects and develops **open-source projects** that help deploy, integrate, and extend SARUS+ in real engineering workflows: utilities, connectors, examples, templates, documentation, and experimental prototypes.

> This is **not** an official vendor organization.  
> The commercial SARUS+ product belongs to its rights holders.  
> Only community open-source work lives here.

Official product website: [sarusplus.ru](http://sarusplus.ru/)

---

## Why this organization exists

SARUS+ is a commercial PLM platform for machinery and instrument engineering (CAD/composites/CAM/PDM).

Around any industrial system a supporting layer appears quickly:

- integrations with adjacent systems and APIs;
- migration and diagnostic scripts;
- data, process, and report templates;
- customization and extension examples;

The goal of this organization is to give that layer a shared home, so projects are not scattered across personal accounts and can be reused.

---

## What belongs here

Open repositories related to the SARUS+ ecosystem are welcome:

- clients, wrappers, and utilities for APIs / data exchange;
- connectors to CAD, ERP, MES, PDM, and related systems;
- tools for migration, reconciliation, and data validation;
- sample models, processes, reports, and configurations;
- documentation, rollout checklists, and training setups;
- UI/UX prototypes, plugins, and helper services.

Requirements:

- an OSI-style open license (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, or similar);
- a clear README: purpose, limitations, how to run;
- an explicit note that the project is **not** part of the commercial product;
- no proprietary vendor code, license keys, or internal enterprise data.

---

## How to propose a project

1. Create a public repository with a license and a short README.
2. Open an issue on this organization profile (or contact the maintainers) with a link and a brief description.
3. If the project fits, it will be transferred or added to the organization.

Pull requests and discussions are welcome.
